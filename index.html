What the draft establishes

The claimed proved bound is 0 ≤ ρ(G,B) − β₂(G) ≤ 1 for every minimum metric basis B of a finite connected simple unweighted subcubic unicyclic graph. A seven-vertex example attains equality. The theorem's scope is unchanged by the exploratory extension.

What it does not establish

The weighted, degree-four and two-cycle samples do not prove a universal generalisation. Three two-cycle cases repeat labelled graphs. The half-unit worst-case noise boundary is generic for connected unit-edge graphs, not a novel empirical discovery. Weighted margins depend on scale; no physical calibration, deployment advantage or improved algorithm is claimed.

The protocol is a local pre-run record, not independently timestamped preregistration. Read the independent computational audit, reproduction guide, provenance and checksums.
